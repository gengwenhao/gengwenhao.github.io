A few things you might wonder

Do I need technical experience?

Everyday use is about choosing cards, adding details, and viewing results. You will need to install the app and connect to an available service first. The beta includes setup instructions.

What can I do with it?

Existing templates cover content creation, link summaries, and business analysis. You can also create custom tasks. Available cards depend on your service and installed skills.

Does the AI run on my phone?

The app is your mobile interface. Your connected service runs the tasks, so starting a task and checking the latest results require an internet connection.

Does it cost anything?

You can request a beta invitation by email. Details will be provided with your invitation. Connected services, models, or third-party tools may charge their own fees.

Does every skill work immediately?

Some skills need additional accounts, models, or tools. Read the description and risk notices before installing, and check that the requirements are met.

Where can I find generated files?

Open the workspace from a task card, or view the files after a conversation finishes. Supported files can be previewed or exported with the share sheet.

Which phones are supported? How do I start?

The iOS beta waitlist is open, but there is no public download yet. Request an invitation by email with your intended task and phone model for further details.
