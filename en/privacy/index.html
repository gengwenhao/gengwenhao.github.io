Privacy policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Information we collect

SkillRunner is a mobile app that connects to task execution services. The app saves connection settings and local preferences, and sends the content required for a task to your connected service.

  • Connection information: service addresses, access tokens, and public keys and signatures used for device pairing
  • Task content: text, links, reference material, and conversation messages you submit
  • Contact information: your email address and message when you request an invitation or send feedback

Data storage

The app stores connection settings, device identity, local preferences, and some cached data on your device. Task conversations and generated files are stored on your connected service. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete server-side conversations, files, or device pairing records. Use the relevant management features or contact the service administrator to remove them.

Third-party services

Your connected service runs tasks and may use model providers, skill sources, or other tools, sending the content needed for a task to those third parties. Processing and retention depend on your service configuration and their privacy policies. Administrators of hosted services may also have access to server-side data.

Network access

SkillRunner accesses the network for the following purposes:

  • Connecting and authenticating with task services, maintaining connections, and synchronizing task cards and conversation status
  • Submitting task content, receiving progress updates, and reading generated files
  • Searching, inspecting, and installing skills, and sharing files at your request

Anyone with a sharing link may be able to access the shared file. Check that its content is appropriate to share, and stop sharing when the link is no longer needed.

Children’s privacy

SkillRunner is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Policy updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.
