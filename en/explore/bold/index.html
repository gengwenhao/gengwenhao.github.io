Before you start

Does AI run on my phone?

The app is your mobile interface. Your connected service executes tasks; an internet connection is required.

Can I download it now?

The iOS beta waitlist is open. There is no public download yet; details come with your invitation.

Where is my data stored?

Task history and files are stored by your connected service. Models or tools may process relevant content. See the privacy policy.
